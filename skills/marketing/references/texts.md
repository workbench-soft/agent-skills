# Texts to adapt

Every text is the user's story: what they built and the reason. Fill in the
brackets, then reword it in the language of the place it is for. Strip any ad
tone.

## Reddit post

```text
Title: I made a free [what it does] for [audience or city], [one hook]

I [built / pulled together] [what]. It [main benefit, one line].

[Second fact a reader can check.] Free, no sign-up. [Where it runs.]

[What's next or what's missing, said up front.] Spotted a bug or a gap? Tell me and I'll fix it.

[link?ref=reddit-<sub>]
```

## Show HN

```text
Title: Show HN: [what it does, plain words]

First comment:
I built [what] because [the pain]. It [how it works, one or two technical facts HN will like: the data source, the stack, a trick].
It's free and [open source / no sign-up]. I'd love feedback on [one specific thing].
```

## Product Hunt

```text
Tagline (60 chars): [what it does for whom]
Maker comment:
Hi, I'm [name]. I made [product] because [the pain]. [What it does in two lines.]
It's free to [core]. [What the paid part adds, if any.] What should it do next?
```

## Forum answer in an old thread

```text
[Answer the question first, with the numbers or the steps.]
I ran into the same thing and built a free [tool] that does this: [link?ref=forum-<site>] (disclosure: it's mine).
```

## Email to a journalist

```text
Subject: [Local hook]: free [what] ahead of [date or event]

Hi [name], I read your piece on [their article]. [Event] starts on [date].
I built a free [what] that [main benefit]. No sign-up: [link]
Happy to send a screenshot for [their area] or answer questions.
[Full name], [city]
```

## Email to a creator or teacher

```text
Subject: Free [paid tier] for [channel name]

Hi [name], your video on [their topic] is how I learned [thing].
I built [product]: [one line]. Here's a code for [paid tier], free for good: [code].
If it's useful to your viewers, an honest review would mean a lot. No pressure either way.
[Name]
```

## Follow-up, once, a week later

```text
Hi [name], a quick follow-up on [product]. [One new fact: a feature, a number of users, a date that is close.] Happy to help if it fits.
```

## Store short description

```text
[What it does] for [who]. [Key benefit]. [Free / no sign-up / works offline].
```
