# Style catalogue

Pick from this list or name a style it lacks. Each line gives the marks that
make a style read at 32 px, where detail is gone.

## Tech and future

- **Cyberpunk** magenta and cyan on near black, a glitch offset of 1 or 2
  units, a hard diagonal cut.
- **Neon** a bright stroke with a blurred copy under it, on a dark tile.
- **Synthwave** a sunset gradient, pink to orange, with a grid floor.
- **Hologram** cyan lines at low opacity, scan lines, a faint glow.
- **Circuit** traces at 45 and 90 degrees, round pads, PCB green or gold.
- **Terminal** a green or amber glyph on black, a block cursor.

## Craft and history

- **Steampunk** brass gradients, gears, rivets, a dark wood or leather tile.
- **Vintage badge** a round or shield badge, 2 muted inks, a thin inner ring.
- **Blueprint** white lines on blueprint blue, a dashed grid, dimension ticks.
- **Woodcut** thick black shapes with carved white lines, one accent ink.
- **Art deco** gold on black, fans, sunbursts, symmetry.

## Flat and modern

- **Flat** 2 to 4 solid colours, no gradient, no stroke.
- **Material** flat shapes with one soft cast shadow and a paper fold.
- **Glassmorphism** a frosted pane over a colour blob, a white edge at 40%.
- **Neumorphism** the tile colour, raised with a light and a dark shadow.
- **Gradient mesh** a bold gradient tile and a white glyph, as on iOS.
- **Line icon** one stroke weight, round caps, no fill.

## Playful and bold

- **Pixel art** a 16 or 32 grid, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, no curves.
- **Isometric** a cube or block at 30 degrees, 3 shades of one hue.
- **Brutalist** black outlines 3 units or thicker, a hard offset shadow, raw
  primaries.
- **Sticker** a white die cut border, a drop shadow, a slight tilt.
- **Clay** soft rounded forms, light top left, a gentle inner shadow.
- **Origami** folded planes, each face a different shade.
- **Low poly** a shape split into triangles, each with its own shade.

## Ink and paint

- **Hand drawn** a wobbly stroke, as if by marker, uneven fills.
- **Watercolour** soft blobs at 60% opacity that overlap, a paper tone.
- **Ukiyo-e** flat inks, a wave or a cloud motif, a red seal.
- **Stained glass** thick dark lead lines between bright panes.

## Match the style to the app

| The app | Styles that fit |
| --- | --- |
| a developer tool, a CLI, a system utility | terminal, circuit, neon, cyberpunk, blueprint, brutalist |
| a game | pixel art, isometric, sticker, clay, low poly |
| notes, a diary, writing | hand drawn, watercolour, line icon, origami |
| money, health, work | flat, material, gradient mesh, glassmorphism |
| photos, music, art | gradient mesh, synthwave, stained glass, ukiyo-e |

- **Spread** take the 10 from at least 3 groups, so the sheet holds real
  choices.
- **Twist** 1 or 2 styles SHOULD be outside the obvious set for the app. A
  surprise is often the pick.
